# Real food photos

Drop your own photographs here and they appear automatically in the menu (nothing to edit in code).
Missing files are simply hidden.

**Category banners** (landscape, ~1600x700 JPG) - shown above each menu list:

`chai.jpg  pizza.jpg  burgers.jpg  pasta.jpg  sandwich.jpg  maggi.jpg  coffee.jpg  drinks.jpg  snacks.jpg  sweets.jpg`

**Item thumbnails** (square, ~400x400 JPG) go in `images/items/` and are named after the item:
lowercase, `&` becomes `and`, other characters become `-`. Examples:

`items/kulhad-chai.jpg  items/margherita.jpg  items/double-patty-cheese.jpg  items/mac-and-cheese.jpg`

Use photos you own or that are licensed for commercial use.
